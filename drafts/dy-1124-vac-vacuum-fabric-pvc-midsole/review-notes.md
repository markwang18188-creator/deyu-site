## Core Positioning

- The article focuses on the manufacturing problem: manual fabric wrapping, adhesive application, fixing and side finishing.
- It explains that vacuum positions the textile before and during PVC injection.
- It does not claim that the entire shoe can be manufactured without adhesive.
- Benefits are described as potential production outcomes that depend on the fabric, mould and process trial.

## External Technical Support

- EP4333666A1 and CN117545396A support the general sequence of using vacuum channels to hold a footwear textile or insert through positioning, mould closing and direct injection.
- US4134955A supports the established use of fabric with injection-moulded thermoplastic footwear and the need to prevent material flow from moving the textile.
- These references explain the general engineering principle; they are not presented as patents owned by Deyu.
