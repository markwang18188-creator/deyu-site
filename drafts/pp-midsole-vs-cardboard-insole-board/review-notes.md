## Editorial Approach

- The article is written as an application comparison, not an absolute claim that PP is always better.
- The 280-400 pairs/hour figure is qualified because mould cavities, part geometry, material, cooling and quality requirements affect actual output.
- "Cardboard" is defined as purpose-made cellulose footwear board, not packaging cardboard.
- PP water resistance, cellulose moisture behaviour and PP bonding difficulty are supported by external technical references.
- Claims about workflow reduction and repeatability are framed as production outcomes that depend on the actual part and process.
